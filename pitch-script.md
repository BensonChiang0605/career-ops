# NextOffer 2-Minute Pitch Script

## 0:00-0:20 - Vision Intro

One of the biggest levers for increasing your income is career mobility. But most people only look for better roles when they’re desperate, so they start from zero.

Imagine an agent that continuously looks for higher-paying, better-fit opportunities. You set it up once, it learns your profile, scans the market, filters the noise, and brings you the roles worth interviewing for.

-----

## 0:20-0:35 - Problem
The problem is not a lack of jobs. It is volume.

If you are open to multiple markets, you can spend hours reading job descriptions, only to find most are not worth applying to, while the few relevant roles are easy to miss.

## 0:35-0:45 - Target Audience
NextOffer is built for ambitious professionals who are open to better roles, but do not have time to constantly monitor the market and evaluate every opportunity manually.

## 0:45-1:05 - Product
NextOffer turns your job search into an AI-powered pipeline. It learns your background, goals, deal-breakers, and proof points, then uses that context to evaluate jobs, rank opportunities, and prepare tailored application materials.

## 1:05-1:40 - Demo Workflow
To set it up, you paste your CV and answer a series of questions from the AI agent. Those answers teach the agent your background, goals, preferences, and constraints. For the sake of time, we are skipping that setup in this demo.

Once everything is configured, you can ask NextOffer to scan the web for roles that fit your profile, analyze a specific job, or prepare an application for you to review and send.

## 1:40-1:55 - Differentiator
The real differentiator is the onboarding flow and agent harness. Technical users could set this up themselves, but for non-technical users, NextOffer makes it easy to pass their context into a series of agents designed to get them their next offer.

Users also decide how hands-on they want to be. They can review every recommendation, or stay hands-off and only get notified when the agent finds a strong match or helps get them an interview.

## 1:55-2:00 - Close
The goal is simple: keep your career options open before you need them, so when the right role appears, you are already ready.
